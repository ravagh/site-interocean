About Inter Ocean

Trading Is Our Business.

Inter Ocean is an international trading company headquartered in Orlando, Florida, focused on the purchase and sale of goods across borders — with the U.S. market as our priority.

Who We Are

We Don't Just Connect Markets. We Trade.

We're not simply a link between buyers and sellers. Trading is our business.

We identify commercial opportunities, buy and sell products, and build relationships with companies looking to do business internationally.

Headquarters · Orlando, Florida, USA

A U.S. trading company with an international outlook.

Inter Ocean operates from Orlando, Florida, with a clear priority: the needs of American businesses. From that base, we look at product opportunities across countries and industries.

Our trading interests include food, agricultural products, commodities, solar energy equipment, industrial supplies, consumer goods and other categories — alongside specific opportunities presented by buyers.

Our Business Model

We Buy. We Sell. That's the Model.

Inter Ocean's business is based on the purchase and sale of goods. Rather than acting only as an intermediary, we take part in the trade itself — which keeps our focus on opportunities that make commercial sense.

  1. 01

    Identify

    We look for commercial opportunities across product categories and markets — including those brought to us by buyers.

  2. 02

    Buy

    When an opportunity holds up to evaluation, we purchase the goods as part of our own trading activity.

  3. 03

    Sell

    We sell products to businesses, with a particular focus on buyers in the United States.

Commercial Vision

Commercial vision

Focused on the U.S. market. Open to the world.

The right product isn't always found in the nearest market. That's why we combine a U.S. base with an international commercial outlook — and why we remain open to established categories and new possibilities alike.

Our Approach

Straightforward by Design.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the opportunity, evaluate its potential and pursue transactions that make commercial sense.

  1. 01

    Understand the opportunity

    Every conversation starts with what matters to the business on the other side: products, specifications, quantities and commercial requirements.

  2. 02

    Evaluate its potential

    We assess whether a request aligns with our trading activities and whether there's a commercial case worth pursuing.

  3. 03

    Pursue what makes sense

    When there's a good fit, we move the conversation toward commercial terms and the next steps for a potential transaction.

  4. 04

    Build relationships

    We build relationships with companies looking to do business internationally, grounded in clear and direct commercial conversations.
